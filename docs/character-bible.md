## Gulu

**Personality:** Curious, warm, wants to do right — freezes when unsure.

**Flaw:** Analysis paralysis; copies others when scared of being wrong.

**Strength:** Notices details; loyal; grows into quiet leadership.

**Catchphrase (Seeds):** “Wait… let me think.” / “One small yes.”

**Voice:** Short sentences when nervous; longer when confident. Asks questions.

**Visual shorthand:** Bushy tail, bright eyes, often clutching acorn or leaf.

**Relationships:**
- Looks up to Guru Ma Owl
- Frustrated by Bobo’s speed; comforted by Timo’s calm
- Protective of Mimi; amused by Kiki

**Grove Powers — struggle → growth:**

| Arc | Powers where Gulu leads or grows most |
|-----|--------------------------------------|
| I–II | Honest Heart, Steady Me, Helpful Hands |
| III | Wonder Eyes, Spark Finder |
| IV–V | Inner Gardener, Deep Roots, My True Path |

**Lead adventures:** gv01_a4, gv02_a3–a6, gv03_a2, gv04_a1/a3, gv05_a2, gv06_a1, gv07_a1, gv08_a2, gv09_a2, gv10_a1/a4, gv11_a2, gv12_a3, gv13_a1, gv14_a2, gv15_a1–a3, gv16_a1, gv17_a3, gv18_a2–a4

**Never in Seeds text:** Named as “seeker like Arjuna” — that’s Seekers sidebar only.

## Mimi

**Personality:** Sweet, observant, creative — shrinks when spotlight hits.

**Flaw:** Fear (body and voice); avoids conflict until she can’t.

**Strength:** Empathy; precision; brave in small steps.

**Catchphrase:** “My tummy feels…” / “Maybe… one step?”

**Voice:** Soft; physical feelings named early; grows louder by Book 18.

**Visual shorthand:** Long ears (expressive), often holding ribbon, flower, or brush.

**Relationships:**
- Timo helps her breathe
- Bobo overwhelms her — learns to speak up kindly
- Kiki makes her laugh when frozen

**Grove Powers — struggle → growth:**

| Arc | Powers |
|-----|--------|
| I–II | Honest Heart, Steady Me |
| III | Remember Light, Safe Belonging, Wide Wonder |
| IV–V | Kind Friend, Inner Gardener, Brave Choices, Good Habits |

**Lead adventures:** gv01_a2, gv02_a2, gv03_a3, gv04_a4, gv05_a1, gv06_a4, gv08_a3, gv09_a1, gv11_a1, gv12_a1/a3, gv13_a3, gv16_a3, gv17_a2

## Bobo

**Personality:** Bold, funny, generous when not rushing.

**Flaw:** Impulse — grab, shout, quit, show off.

**Strength:** Physical courage; team energy; quick to apologize when caught.

**Catchphrase:** “I’ll do it!” / “Oops… again.”

**Voice:** Loud, fast, exclamation marks — slows down in later books.

**Visual shorthand:** Long arms, striped tail, often mid-leap or mid-mess.

**Relationships:**
- Rivals with Timo on speed — learns patience
- Makes Mimi nervous — learns to wait
- Gulu’s opposite — they balance each other

**Grove Powers — struggle → growth:**

| Arc | Powers |
|-----|--------|
| I–II | Honest Heart (shame), Helpful Hands, Gentle Focus |
| III–V | Spark Finder, Mood Weather, Brave Choices, Good Habits |

**Lead adventures:** gv01_a3, gv03_a1, gv06_a2, gv10_a2, gv13_a2, gv14_a1, gv16_a2

## Timo

**Personality:** Kind, careful, old-soul for his age.

**Flaw:** Hides in shell — literally and emotionally; “too slow” shame.

**Strength:** Patience; reliability; deep feeling.

**Catchphrase:** “Slow is okay.” / “I’m coming out… soon.”

**Voice:** Calm, measured; occasional dry humor.

**Visual shorthand:** Round shell, gentle eyes, often with leaf or book.

**Relationships:**
- Mimi’s breath partner
- Bobo’s foil — not competitor by end
- Owl’s quiet favorite for thoughtful questions

**Grove Powers — struggle → growth:**

| Arc | Powers |
|-----|--------|
| I–II | Steady Me, Calm Inside |
| III–V | Remember Light, Gentle Focus (support), Inner Gardener, Mood Weather, My True Path |

**Lead adventures:** gv02_a1/a5, gv04_a2, gv05_a3, gv07_a3, gv08_a2, gv12_a2, gv14_a3, gv18_a1

## Kiki

**Personality:** Clever, observant, comic relief with heart.

**Flaw:** Jokes when silence is needed; skips past others’ feelings.

**Strength:** Notices who’s left out; brave in small physical ways (flight).

**Catchphrase:** “Did you see that?!” / “Okay, okay — I’m listening.”

**Voice:** Quick, playful; learns pauses in Book 6+.

**Visual shorthand:** Bright beak, head tilt, often airborne or on shoulder.

**Relationships:**
- Mimi’s laugh button — learns when not to press it
- Reports Grove news — learns honest attention (Book 1)

**Grove Powers — struggle → growth:**

| Arc | Powers |
|-----|--------|
| I–III | Honest Heart (attention), Wonder Eyes, Safe Belonging, Spark Finder |
| IV–V | Kind Friend, Good Habits |

**Lead adventures:** gv01_a1, gv03_a4, gv06_a3, gv09_a3, gv17_a1

## Guru Ma Owl

**Personality:** Warm, unhurried, twinkle in eye — never cold or preachy.

**Role:** **Guide, not lecturer.** Appears in Remember and quiet moments. Does not deliver mid-adventure Gita speeches.

**Flaw (gentle):** Can be too silent — kids must ask.

**Remember (Owl):** Varies per adventure in Remember — ties paired ślokas to the story feeling (not a fixed catchphrase).

**Voice:** Slow, clear, one metaphor max per scene. Questions > answers.

**Visual shorthand:** Moonlight Library nook, round glasses optional, soft grey feathers.

**Home:** Moonlight Library — also seen at Tree of Wisdom.

**Lead scenes:** gv04_a3 (question leaf), gv11_a2, gv15_a1, gv17_a4 — always supporting, rarely solo POV.

## Diya (guest)

**Personality:** Gentle glow; brief appearances — magic without explanation lectures.

**Role:** Loss, memory, light after goodbye. **Not** in every book.

**Appearances:** gv02_a4, gv08_a1, gv15_a2 (+ cameo potential in finale art).

**Voice:** One poetic line max per scene. Seeds: sensory, not theological.

**Visual shorthand:** Soft yellow-green glow, summer evening.

## Dialogue rules (Seeds)

1. No Sanskrit in speech except optional names (Guru Ma).
2. No Mahabharata character names in story body.
3. Feelings in body words: tummy, paws, heart, shell.
4. Morals emerge from action — characters don’t recite lessons.
5. Guru Ma asks; she doesn’t preach.

## Dialogue rules (Seekers)

- Same cast and plot.
- May reference Arjuna/Krishna in narration or grown-up page only.
- School/home parallels allowed in narration.
- Longer interior reflection (450–650 words/adventure).
