## North star

**Gita Grove** is a persistent world where animal friends grow **18 Grove Powers** through ordinary-extraordinary days — fair setup, bridge repair, rainy week, night sky. Gita wisdom lives in **Remember** and grown-up pages, not in chapter-shaped homework.

**Brand line (internal):** *Grow your powers in the Grove.*

---

## The map

Seven locations + one central Tree. Locations are **training grounds**, not curriculum chapters.

```
                    [ Moonlight Library ]
                            |
        [ Courage Hill ] ---+--- [ Blossom Meadow ]
                \           |           /
                 [ Tree of Wisdom ]
                /           |           \
   [ River of Patience ] ---+--- [ Kindness Garden ]
                            |
                   [ Rainbow Bridge ]
```

**Travel rule:** Friends walk between locations in one afternoon — Grove is small enough for ages 5–7 to hold in mind.

**No battlefield, no weapons, no villain nations.** Conflict is internal (fear, rush, shame) and relational (fight, exclude, quit).

## Locations (detail)

### Tree of Wisdom

- **Center** of Grove; visible from every location.
- **Function:** Celebrate, reflect, series finales, Remember ritual.
- **Visual:** Wide trunk, low branches kids can climb, leaves added per completed adventure.
- **Progression:** 5 branch colors (arcs) · ~68 leaves total at series end.
- **Not:** A lecture hall; Owl doesn’t “teach Gita” here to crowds.

### Courage Hill

- Steep paths, starting lines, small stage, ribbon poles.
- **Powers:** Honest Heart, Steady Me, Brave Choices, Wide Wonder (night).
- **Scenarios:** Sports day, ceremony, speaking up, stargazing.

### Blossom Meadow

- Flowers, game circles, fair booths, open sky.
- **Powers:** Helpful Hands, Spark Finder, Good Habits, Mood Weather (festival).
- **Scenarios:** Spring Fair, drum circles, color days, festivals.

### Rainbow Bridge

- Arched bridge over stream; team crossings, wobbly boards.
- **Powers:** Helpful Hands, Kind Friend, Safe Belonging, Leadership.
- **Scenarios:** Repair crew, path arguments, new neighbors on bench.

### Kindness Garden

- Vegetables, fruit trees, sharing bench, compost, ant paths.
- **Powers:** Helpful Hands, Kind Friend, Inner Gardener, Good Habits.
- **Scenarios:** Sharing food, planting, sorry gifts, lunch fair.

### River of Patience

- Slow water, stepping stones, paper boats, lotus at edge.
- **Powers:** Calm Inside, Gentle Focus, Mood Weather, Devotion (letting go).
- **Scenarios:** Rainy week, waiting for green, boat release, wobble stones.

### Moonlight Library

- Owl’s nook — books, maps, question bowl, soft lamps.
- **Powers:** Learning Heart, Remember Light, Deep Roots, Gentle Focus.
- **Scenarios:** Lost book, story hour, firefly path home, bark drawings.

## Learning loop (every adventure)

| Step | Where | What happens |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Adventure opens** | Location | Problem teased in Grove daily life |
| **Story** | Location | Friends live the struggle; flaw meets sub-skill |
| **Peak** | Location | Feeling strongest — fear, shame, rush |
| **Resolution** | Location | Kind, calm, brave outcome (not perfect) |
| **Celebrate** | Tree or location | Name the **Grove Power** grown |
| **Practice** | Activity pages | Draw, maze, role-play sub-skill |
| **Remember** | End of module | Listen + optional repeat; Owl bridge line |
| **Grown-up** | One page | Power + question + optional verse refs |

**Removed from v1 app alignment:** Separate “Source tab” before story — story first, Remember last.

## Tree of Wisdom — progress system

### Branch colors (by arc)

| Arc | Books | Branch color | Kid name |
|-----|-------|--------------|----------|
| I | 1–2 | Soft gold | When life feels too big |
| II | 3–6 | Leaf green | How we show up |
| III | 7–11 | Sky blue | How we see the world |
| IV | 12–15 | Blossom pink | Who we are inside |
| V | 16–18 | Deep bark brown | How we live forward |

### Leaves

- **One leaf** per completed adventure (`gv##_a#`).
- Leaf icon may show tiny location glyph (optional app UX).
- **Book completion:** branch segment fills; Owl line in Celebrate.
- **Series completion (Book 18):** full bloom; reader leaf to color/cut.

### App sync (future `feature/gita-grove`)

- QR on book → deep link adventure → leaf on Tree.
- Progress keyed by `gvId`, not legacy `sd*`.
- Parent dashboard: powers grown, not “chapters completed.”

## Rules of the world

1. **No death on page** — caterpillar becomes moth; goodbye ≠ gone forever when framed with care.
2. **No human children** in Grove — animal cast only (human readers mirror themselves).
3. **Guru Ma never solves the plot** — friends act; Owl witnesses.
4. **Diya appears sparingly** — wonder guest, not deus ex machina every book.
5. **Seeds:** zero Mahabharata naming in story body.
6. **Seekers:** Arjuna/Krishna in sidebar or narration only.
7. **Titles:** moments and objects — not verse vocabulary.
8. **Economics:** print = 80–100 pp @ ~₹199; 25 pp = internal module.

## What Grove is NOT

- Not a chapter-by-chapter Gita course for kids.
- Not Mahabharata retelling with animal cosplay.
- Not 120 disconnected moral one-offs (anthology Meera/Rohan style retired).
- Not verse-first homework (“today we learn 2.47”).
