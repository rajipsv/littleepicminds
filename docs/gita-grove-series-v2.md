# Gita Grove — Series v2 (Adventure Catalog)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Companion:** [`gita-grove-capabilities.md`](gita-grove-capabilities.md)  
**Scope:** All 18 books · ~68 adventures · scenario synopses (not final manuscripts)

Each entry: Grove Power → sub-skill → organic plot. Titles are moment-based. Ślokas paired **after** manuscript draft (Remember only).

## Arc I — When life feels too big (Books 1–2)

### Book 1 · Honest Heart · *When the Heart Trembles* (~88 pp · 4 adventures)

**Season hook:** Spring Fair week — the Grove is loud before the drum sounds. Each friend learns honesty looks different.

| ID | Title | Location | Lead | Sub-skill | Synopsis |
|----|-------|----------|------|-----------|----------|
| gv01_a1 | **The Fair Before the Drum** | Blossom Meadow | Kiki | Honest attention | Setup day for Spring Fair. Everyone runs toward noise and color. Kiki almost joins — then spots a tiny field mouse friend sitting alone on the wooden steps, too scared to come down. Kiki sits beside them instead of flying ahead. No sermon — just company until the mouse takes one step. |
| gv01_a2 | **Mimi's Butterflies** | Courage Hill | Mimi | Honest body | Mimi is chosen to hold the opening ribbon. Her paws shake; her tummy flips. She wants to hide behind Timo. Timo teaches a silly breath game (smell the flower, blow the candle). Mimi still feels scared — and walks to the hill anyway. |
| gv01_a3 | **The Banner in the Mud** | Rainbow Bridge | Bobo | Honest disappointment | Bobo grabs the team banner and sprints across the bridge. He slips; mud splashes; everyone laughs. Bobo laughs too — then walks away and cries where he thinks no one sees. Friends find him and sit in the mud. Nobody fixes it; they stay. |
| gv01_a4 | **Under the Quiet Tree** | Tree of Wisdom | Gulu | Honest surrender | Gulu still has no role in the fair. He sits under the Tree, quiet. Guru Ma Owl doesn’t explain life — she listens. Gulu says, “I don’t know what to do.” Owl says, “That’s honest.” Gulu chooses one small yes: carry water for the flower table. |

**Book bridge:** Tree gets four leaves — one per adventure. Celebrate: “Honest Heart grows when we tell the truth about feelings.”

**Season hook:** After the fair, ordinary days test whether friends bounce back or stay stuck.

| ID | Title | Location | Lead | Sub-skill | Synopsis |
|----|-------|----------|------|-----------|----------|

| gv02_a2 | **The Wrong Note** | Moonlight Library | Mimi | Wise sadness | Mimi practices a song for Grove Night. One wrong note; she stops and wants to quit forever. Owl hums the wrong note on purpose, grins, and keeps going. Mimi tries again — not perfect, finished. |
| gv02_a3 | **Leaves Everywhere** | Kindness Garden | Gulu | Things pass | Workers trim Gulu’s favorite climbing branch for safety. Gulu is furious, then heartbroken. Leaves fall like rain. He rakes them angry, then rakes them with Timo — same garden, new season. |
| gv02_a4 | **The Light That Stayed** | Moonlight Library | Gulu + Diya | Light stays | Gulu’s caterpillar friend wraps in a cocoon. Days pass; Gulu waits, worried. **Diya** the firefly visits at dusk — “Some goodbyes are helloes in a coat.” Cocoon opens; moth flies; Gulu waves. |
| gv02_a5 | **Hand-Me-Down Day** | Blossom Meadow | Timo | Change like coat | Timo wears cousin’s old festival vest — too big, then just right. He feels small inside it, then proud. Same Timo, new cloth. |
| gv02_a6 | **The Day the Finish Line Moved** | Courage Hill | Gulu | Try without clutching | Sports day relay. Gulu is winning — Mimi trips ahead. He stops, helps her up, they finish last together. Coach claps loudest for that. |
| gv02_a7 | **Boats We Don't Chase** | River of Patience | All | Let go peacefully | Friends release paper boats with wishes. Current takes them. Bobo wants to run downstream and grab his back. They watch together until boats vanish — peaceful, not empty. |

**Book bridge:** Arc I closes — gold branch on Tree complete. “Steady Me means feelings change; you can keep going kindly.”
