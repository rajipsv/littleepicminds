# Book 1 · Honest Heart — Adventure Hooks

**Version:** 1.0  
**Book:** *When the Heart Trembles* · Spring Fair week  
**Purpose:** Page-25 teasers + opening beats so each module feels like one serial week, not four isolated stories.

---

## Rules (Seeds)

1. **Teaser = feeling + question**, not a lesson recap.
2. **Name the next lead** and one body/image detail (shaking paws, muddy tail, empty list).
3. **Same clock** — all four adventures happen during Spring Fair week (setup → opening → parade → quiet day before finale).
4. **Page 25** = title of next adventure + 3–4 short lines + italic wonder question.
5. **Page 2 or 3** of the *next* adventure may echo the teaser in one line (optional `openingHook`).

---

## Season frame (compiled book intro · ~6 pp)

> Spring Fair was coming to Gita Grove.
>
> Hammers, ribbons, drums under cloth — and four friends who each had something true to say, if they could find the words (or the breath, or the courage to stay).

**Tree visual:** Empty Honest Heart branch — room for four leaves.

## Adventure chain

### gv01_a1 → gv01_a2

**After:** Kiki stays beside Pip; fair setup ends; drum still covered.

**Page 25 teaser** *(copy for layout page 25)*

> **Next adventure:** *Mimi's Butterflies*
>
> Spring Fair opens at sunrise tomorrow. The red cloth comes off the great drum — and Mimi the rabbit has been chosen to hold the opening ribbon at Courage Hill.
>
> Her paws are already shaking. Her tummy feels full of butterflies.
>
> *Can you walk up a hill when your whole body says wait?*

**Art tease:** Mimi at bottom of Courage Hill, ribbon in paws, sun rising; drum cloth in far background.

**gv01_a2 opening hook** *(pages 2–3)*

> Sunrise painted Courage Hill gold. Mimi stood at the bottom with the ribbon in her paws — and every butterfly in her tummy wanted to fly away.

---

### gv01_a2 → gv01_a3

**After:** Mimi walks to the hill scared but walking; crowd cheered; fair is officially open.

**Page 25 teaser**

> **Next adventure:** *The Banner in the Mud*
>
> The ribbon fluttered. The crowd cheered. Now it was the friends' turn to carry the Grove team banner across Rainbow Bridge.
>
> Bobo grabbed the pole first. "I'll lead!" he shouted — and ran.
>
> *What happens when the mud wins?*

**Art tease:** Bobo mid-sprint with banner pole; slick wet planks on Rainbow Bridge.

**gv01_a3 opening hook**

> Rainbow Bridge was slippery after morning rain. Bobo didn't notice. He had the banner, the wind, and a very fast plan.

---

### gv01_a3 → gv01_a4

**After:** Friends sit with Bobo in the mud; banner rescued; laughter fades into afternoon.

**Page 25 teaser**

> **Next adventure:** *Under the Quiet Tree*
>
> The banner dried in the sun. Bobo washed his face. Mimi hummed. Kiki chattered.
>
> But Gulu the squirrel still had no job on the fair list — not one line with his name.
>
> He walked to the Tree of Wisdom and sat down without a word.
>
> *What do you do when you don't know what to do?*

**Art tease:** Gulu small under the great Tree; fair bustle distant; empty sign-up clipboard.

**gv01_a4 opening hook**

> The fair list was full of names — Mimi, Bobo, Kiki, Timo, Pip. Gulu read it twice. His name wasn't there.

---

### gv01_a4 → book close (and series)

**After:** Gulu carries water for the flower table; one small yes; fair continues.

**Page 25 teaser** *(last module — book bridge, not next SKU)*

> **Honest Heart — four leaves on the Tree**
>
> Kiki noticed someone small. Mimi breathed and walked. Bobo let friends see his tears. Gulu said, "I don't know" — and found one yes.
>
> Four friends. Four honest moments. One Spring Fair week.
>
> *The Grove keeps growing. Next time, feelings don't go away overnight...*
>
> **Coming in Book 2:** *Timo Won't Come Out*

**Art tease:** Tree branch with four new leaves; six friends + Guru Ma Owl silhouetted at dusk.

**Book bridge line** *(4 pp at compile time)*

> "Honest Heart grows when we tell the truth about feelings."
