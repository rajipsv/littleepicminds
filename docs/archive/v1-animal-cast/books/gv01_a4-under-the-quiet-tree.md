## Moral

### Moral

You can say "I don't know" out loud — and still choose one small yes today.

**Child repeat line:** "I can say I don't know."

---

## Practice activities

### Practice

1. **Honest pause** — When you don't know what to do, say it: *"I don't know yet."* Take one breath before deciding.

### Practice

2. **One small yes** — Pick one tiny helpful thing (carry a cup, hold a door, water a plant). Do it once.

### Practice

3. **Quiet tree** — Sit under a real tree or in a quiet corner for one minute. Notice three sounds far away.
