### Page 3 — Opens

**Image prompt:** Wide Blossom Meadow, Spring Fair prep day. Flower tents, hammer sounds implied, Gulu squirrel stacking seed boxes near empty stage, Kiki myna bird zooming between tents. Establishing shot, busy joyful atmosphere

Tomorrow was Spring Fair in Gita Grove.

Blossom Meadow had never been so busy. Hammers tap-tapped. Flower strings flapped in the wind. Someone smelled like fresh mango juice and wet grass.

Kiki the myna bird zoomed from tent to tent. "Almost ready! Almost ready!" she sang.

Gulu the squirrel stacked seed boxes near the empty stage. His tail twitched with excitement.

### Page 4 — Opens

**Image prompt:** Bobo monkey swinging on rope, Mimi rabbit with ribbon rolls, Timo tortoise with nail bucket, all moving toward covered fair drum on stage. Friends mid-task, motion toward big noise

Bobo the monkey swung from a rope, hollering, "Faster! Faster! The drum test is coming!"

Mimi the rabbit balanced ribbon rolls in her paws, eyes wide and happy.

Timo the tortoise carried nails in a small bucket, one careful step at a time.

Everyone was moving toward the **big noise** — the place where the opening drum would soon boom.

### Page 5 — Almost joining

**Image prompt:** Kiki myna bird flapping toward fair stage, red cloth covering great drum, excited expression. Low angle toward stage, sense of pull toward loud fun

Kiki loved big noise. Big noise meant fun. Big noise meant friends.

She flapped toward the stage where a red cloth covered the great fair drum.

### Page 6 — Almost joining

**Image prompt:** Close-up Kiki pausing mid-flight, one small squeak interrupting. Contrast between big exciting meadow blur and tiny quiet moment. Minimal background detail

Then — a tiny squeak.

Not loud. Not exciting.

Just… small.

### Page 7 — Pip on the steps

**Image prompt:** Pip field mouse alone on wooden steps below stage, round ears, paws tucked, watching colorful meadow swirl. Kiki landing on rail above. Drum cloth visible in background

On the wooden steps below the stage sat Pip, a field mouse with round ears and paws tucked tight.

Pip watched the meadow swirl with color. Pip did not move.

Kiki landed on the rail. "Come on! You'll miss the ribbon test!"

### Page 8 — Pip on the steps

**Image prompt:** Eye-level Kiki on step rail beside Pip, Kiki stopped mid-joke, Pip trembling with shiny eyes. Intimate two-shot, meadow activity soft in background

Pip shook. "Too many paws. Too many voices. My heart goes thump-thump-thump."

Kiki opened her beak to joke. She wanted to say something silly to fix it.

But Pip's eyes were shiny. Kiki stopped.

### Page 9 — Honest attention

**Image prompt:** Kiki folded wings sitting beside Pip on steps, not above or in front. Bobo blurred zipping past. Equal eye level, Honest Heart moment — choosing to stay

Bobo zipped by again. "Kiki! Hurry!"

Kiki's wings itched to fly.

Instead, she folded them and sat on the step beside Pip — not in front, not above. **Beside.**

"Is your heart still thump-thumping?" Kiki asked.

### Page 10 — Honest attention

**Image prompt:** Kiki and Pip talking on steps, honest expressions. Kiki admitting fear of missing Pip. Warm quiet beat, fair bustle distant

Pip nodded. "I'm scared of the drum. And scared of missing everything. Both at once."

Kiki had never said that out loud, but she knew the feeling. Sometimes fun and scary came in the same box.

"Me too, a little," Kiki said. Honest.

Pip looked surprised. "You? You're never scared."

"I'm scared of missing **you**," Kiki said.

### Page 11 — One step

**Image prompt:** Kiki and Pip sitting on steps while Gulu, Mimi, Timo, Bobo pass in background with cups and ribbons. Stillness vs bustle contrast

They sat while Gulu ran past with cups. While Mimi dropped one ribbon and laughed. While Timo called, "Slow down, Bobo!"

The meadow kept buzzing. The drum stayed covered.

### Page 12 — One step

**Image prompt:** Pip taking one small step down wooden step, Kiki matching pace beside — not rushing. Side view, one step at a time

Pip took one breath. Then one step down.

Kiki took one step too — matching, not rushing.

### Page 13 — Resolution

**Image prompt:** Pip and Kiki reach grass level, Pip still trembling but walking. Gulu noticing and inviting Pip to sort cups. Gentle relief

By the time they reached the grass, Pip was still trembling — but walking.

Gulu noticed. "Oh! Pip! Want to help me sort cups? Quiet job."

Pip nodded fast.

### Page 14 — Resolution

**Image prompt:** Split focus: Kiki glancing up at busy stage with covered drum, then looking down at Pip sorting cups with Gulu. Kiki choosing to stay low one more minute

Kiki flew up once, looked at the busy stage, looked at Pip sorting cups with careful paws.

She chose to stay low for one more minute.

### Page 15 — Resolution

**Image prompt:** Quiet triumphant moment — Pip sorting cups, Kiki hovering low and smiling. Soft celebration, brave minute together, fair prep continues around them

That minute felt like its own kind of brave.

## Moral

### Page 16 — Moral

Before you run toward the loud fun, look around — someone small might need you beside them.

**Child repeat line:** "I can pause and notice."

---

## Practice activities

### Page 20 — Practice

1. **Draw what you see** — Empty fair stage before the crowd arrives. Who is in your picture besides you?

### Page 21 — Practice

2. **Pause game** — When a grown-up says "Fair drum!" freeze for 3 seconds and look who is near you.

### Page 22 — Practice

3. **One step** — With a friend, take one slow step together across the room. Match pace, don't rush.
