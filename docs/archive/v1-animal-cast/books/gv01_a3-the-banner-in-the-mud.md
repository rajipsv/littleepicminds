## Moral

### Moral

You can let disappointment show — sad and mad can sit together. Friends don't have to fix it; they can stay beside you.

**Child repeat line:** "I can let my sad show."

---

## Practice activities

### Practice

1. **Mud sit** — With a friend, sit on the floor for one minute. No fixing, no jokes — just stay.

### Practice

2. **Two feelings** — Name two feelings at once: *"I'm sad AND mad"* or *"I'm tired AND excited."* Both can be true.

### Practice

3. **Slow crossing** — Walk across a room together at half speed. Match pace. No racing.
