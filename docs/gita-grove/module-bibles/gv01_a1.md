## One-line logline

When a Grove Club day piles up, Vanshi learns she can pause with Aarav beside her.

## Beats (numbered 1–13)

1. **Opening hook** — Grove Club planned a small celebration under the Old Banyan — decorations, games, and a shared snack table. Three things go wrong before anyone sits down.

2. **Goal** — Help the day feel special for everyone.

3. **Obstacle** — Wind, a spilled rangoli, and a missing snack bowl hit at once.

4. **Emotional complication** — Vanshi's blind spot: takes things personally.

5. **Wrong choice** — Vanshi tries to fix everything alone and snaps when Aarav asks a question.

6. **Consequence** — Her words land sharp; a younger child goes quiet.

7. **Escalation** — More helpers arrive asking what to do — the noise grows.

8. **Turning point** — Aarav does not argue. He lists only two things. "Pick one. We do that first."

9. **Discovery** — Overwhelmed is a real feeling — not failure.

10. **Gita moment placement** — Sometimes everything hits at once and your body feels too full to move.

11. **Application** — Vanshi names it out loud: "This is too much." She chooses one small task.

12. **Resolution** — The group finishes one thing together; the rest can wait.

13. **Growth shown** — She asks for help before the next pile-up.
