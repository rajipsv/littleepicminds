## One-line logline

Mira must choose how to help on the playground while Vihaan shows her both sides matter.

## Beats (numbered 1–13)

1. **Opening hook** — Two classmates need help at once — one dropped lunch, one sits alone after a rough game.

2. **Goal** — Help quickly because that is what Mira does.

3. **Obstacle** — Both needs are real; acting fast might miss the quieter one.

4. **Emotional complication** — Mira's blind spot: rushes before thinking.

5. **Wrong choice** — Mira runs to the louder problem and waves off the quiet child.

6. **Consequence** — The quiet child walks away; Vihaan watches, worried.

7. **Escalation** — Mira finishes the first fix but feels something unfinished.

8. **Turning point** — Vihaan asks, "Who did we not see?" — not accusing, wondering.

9. **Discovery** — A hard choice means someone waits — and seeing that is part of choosing.

10. **Gita moment placement** — When two good actions pull you, the choice itself feels heavy.

11. **Application** — Mira goes back, sits beside the quiet child, shares her snack.

12. **Resolution** — She could not fix both at once — but she did not pretend the second need was gone.

13. **Growth shown** — She pauses one breath before sprinting.
