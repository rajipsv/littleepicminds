## Beats (numbered 1–13)

1. **Opening hook** — At School, after-school club runs long and something ordinary turns tricky. Mira notices responsibility first — Aarav is nearby.

2. **Goal** — Mira wants the situation to feel fair and finished today.

3. **Obstacle** — Learn to distinguish what should and should not be done. Responsibility makes the usual fix harder.

4. **Emotional complication** — Mira's blind spot: rushes before thinking.

5. **Wrong choice** — Mira defaults to their habit (rushes before thinking) and pushes past Aarav's slower idea.

6. **Consequence** — Someone feels unseen or the mess gets bigger.

7. **Escalation** — Others arrive, or a deadline moves closer.

8. **Turning point** — Aarav slows the moment: Action guided by understanding

9. **Discovery** — Learn to distinguish what should and should not be done — saying it plainly helps more than fixing everything alone.

10. **Gita moment placement** — Late in the story, Mira names the feeling behind responsibility in simple words.

11. **Application** — Mira tries one honest, smaller step with Aarav beside them.

12. **Resolution** — The day ends imperfect but true; the Grove keeps breathing.

13. **Growth shown** — Mira repeats the new move once — action, not a lesson speech.
