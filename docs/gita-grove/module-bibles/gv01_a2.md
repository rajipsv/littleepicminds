## One-line logline

Aarav cannot choose a Grove Journal page layout until Kabir helps him try one small start.

## Beats (numbered 1–13)

1. **Opening hook** — The Grove Journal needs a new page for the club wall. Aarav has four layout ideas and keeps erasing.

2. **Goal** — Finish one page the group can use.

3. **Obstacle** — Every option seems wrong once he imagines what could fail.

4. **Emotional complication** — Aarav's blind spot: overthinks and freezes.

5. **Wrong choice** — He redraws until the paper tears and says he cannot do it.

6. **Consequence** — Kabir waits, then wanders toward the window — the wall stays blank.

7. **Escalation** — Club meeting starts in twenty minutes.

8. **Turning point** — Kabir spreads three objects on the table. "What if we just pick one corner and go?"

9. **Discovery** — Not knowing is honest — starting small is allowed.

10. **Gita moment placement** — When you cannot see the next step, your mind can spin in circles.

11. **Application** — Aarav picks one corner, one question, one drawing.

12. **Resolution** — The page is imperfect and done.

13. **Growth shown** — He says "I do not know yet" without shame.
