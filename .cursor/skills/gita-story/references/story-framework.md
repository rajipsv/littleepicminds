# Story framework — 13 beats + 7 stages

## Seven stages (creative)

1. **Spark** — something happens in ordinary world  
2. **Want** — child wants something  
3. **Struggle** — obstacle + emotional complication  
4. **Inner knot** — real problem is inside (blind spot)  
5. **Choice** — character chooses how to respond  
6. **Discovery** — Gita principle meaningful through experience  
7. **Transformation** — behaves differently without being lectured  

## Thirteen beats (Grove module bible)

| # | Beat | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | Opening hook | Never "today we learn…" |
| 2 | Goal | What lead wants |
| 3 | Obstacle | External challenge |
| 4 | Emotional complication | Archetype blind spot |
| 5 | Wrong choice | Realistic mistake |
| 6 | Consequence | Something actually happens |
| 7 | Escalation | Stakes rise |
| 8 | Turning point | New way of seeing |
| 9 | Discovery | Idea felt, not announced |
| 10 | Gita moment placement | Late only · feeling named · no verse yet |
| 11 | Application | Lead tries new response |
| 12 | Resolution | Adventure closes |
| 13 | Growth shown | Behavior change in action |

## Example (BG 2.47 pattern)

**Premise:** Aarav enters school garden competition. Imagines trophy. Compares plants. Forgets to water. Plant wilts.

**Turn:** Tara asks: *"What does your plant need right now?"*

**Discovery:** *"Water. Not a trophy."*

**Transformation:** Cares for plant; winning becomes secondary.

**Gita connection (later, learning skill):** *We can choose our effort. We cannot control every result.*

## Page map hint (~12 story spreads)

Map beats 1–13 across pages 3–14 in clusters — not one beat per page unless climax spread.

## Quality questions (story-only)

- Enjoyable with zero Gita knowledge?
- Lead's blind spot drives the mistake?
- Ending shows change in **action**?
- Supporting character didn't "fix" the insight too fast?
