# Pedagogy — story layer

Story teaches through **experience**, not explanation.

## Loop (world bible aligned)

```text
Experience → Emotion → Mistake → Consequence → Reflection → Wisdom → Application
```

**Never:** śloka → lecture → story.

## Show vs tell

| Tell (avoid) | Show (prefer) |
|--------------|---------------|
| "Aarav learned not to worry about results" | Aarav waters the plant, smiling at the flower |
| "Vanshi felt overwhelmed" | Vanshi's hands full, voice small, steps backward |
| "Kindness is important" | Mira shares her last bandage without being asked |

## Supporting character role

- Mirror · question · small help · witness
- **Not** the wisdom delivery system
- Tara may ask one grounding question — lead must **discover**

## Emotion authenticity

- Disappointment, jealousy, fear are allowed — resolve with care
- No trauma plots · no punishment-as-lesson
- Mistakes have **natural** consequences ( wilted plant, hurt friend, unfinished project)

## Gita embedding

The teaching must be **removable in theory, invisible in practice**:

- Reader feels: *"Oh — I don't have to control everything"*  
- Not: *"This story was about verse 2.47"*
