# Age 5–10 — shared audience rules

**Canonical copy.** Referenced by `gita-story`, `gita-picture-book`, `gita-learning`, and `gita-quality`.

Gita Grove uses **one unified voice band** for story, book, and learning layers unless the user explicitly requests a Seeds/Seekers print split.

## Target

Children **5–10 years**. A 5-year-old can follow the plot; a 10-year-old can still enjoy and think about it.

## Language

- Simple but **not babyish**
- Warm, emotionally engaging, concrete
- Present tense or simple past · short sentences · dialogue allowed
- Accessible phrasing: *"I can try my best"* — not *"attachment to fruits of action"*
- **Do not distort** the underlying Gita principle to simplify

## Experiences to draw from

Friendship · sharing · trying · patience · mistakes · courage · kindness · disappointment · jealousy · fairness · responsibility · perseverance · fear · comparison · choices · handling emotions · helping others · success and failure

## Word budgets (Grove modules)

| Layer | Budget |
|-------|--------|
| Story spread | 40–60 words EN |
| Moral + repeat line | 1 sentence + 8–12 word repeat |
| Remember child meaning | Plain English, no jargon |
| Grown-up page | 80–120 words |
| Practice activities | Doable at home/school without special materials |

## Tone rules

- Curious, not preachy
- Show change through **action**, not lecture
- Supporting character may ask a question — avoid fixing the lead's problem instantly
- No Arjuna/Krishna names in **story body** (grown-up page may cite)

## Print tiers (optional overlay)

| Tier | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Seeds** (default module) | 25-page adventure · softer naming in metadata |
| **Seekers** | Same adventure ID · deeper grown-up layer · may name Arjuna/Krishna in overlay text |

When both exist, **story prose stays one 5–10 band**; Seekers adds depth in back matter only.

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Two separate story versions for younger vs older within 5–10
- ❌ Academic philosophy vocabulary in child-facing text
- ❌ Talking down ("you're just a little kid")
- ❌ Moral announced before the adventure earns it
