# Guru Ma Remember lines

**Where:** Remember section only (pages 17–19). **Never** mid-story lecture.

## Formula

1. Open: **In the Bhagavad Gita, from India's puranas** (or *one of India's great puranas*)
2. One–three sentences: scene/meaning of **paired ślokas** in plain English
3. Close: tie to child's adventure feeling (not "this story was about verse X")

## Agent knowledge

You should know Bhagavad Gita semantics. Match Guru Ma line to the **actual** meaning of assigned verses — e.g.:

| Verses | Core meaning |
|--------|----------------|
| 1.1–1.2 | Dhritarashtra asks what happened on dharmakṣetra; Sanjaya begins |
| 1.28–1.29 | Arjuna's compassion; body symptoms (limbs, mouth, trembling) |
| 1.30–1.31 | Bow slips; mind reels; sees no good in fighting |
| 1.46–1.47 | Sets down bow; overwhelmed; refuses to fight / confusion |

## Seeds vs grown-up

| Layer | Names |
|-------|-------|
| Guru Ma line (child-facing) | Roles OK ("blind king", "great warrior"); avoid long Sanskrit |
| Grown-up page | May cite Dhritarashtra, Arjuna, Kṛṣṇa, verse refs |

## Pairing new adventures

1. Finish story draft
2. Choose 2 ślokas from the book's Gita chapter pool (capabilities doc parent note)
3. Write Guru Ma line from verse **meaning**, not keyword match
4. Set `shlokas`, `guruMaLine`, `shlokasStatus: "paired"` in curriculum

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ "Wise hearts have sung this feeling in an old song"
- ❌ Same line on every adventure
- ❌ Śloka text driving the plot title
- ❌ Guru Ma explaining the moral the child already saw in the story
