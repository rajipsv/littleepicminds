# Activity template

## Naming

| Layer | Grove section | Prompt style |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| Notice | Practice 1–2 | "When do I feel…?" · "Where do I see…?" |
| Try | Practice 3 | One small action today |
| Reflect | Celebrate + grown-up | "What happened when…?" |

## Rules (ages 5–10)

- Doable at home or school · no special materials required
- Tied to **theme**, not śloka recitation
- Concrete · one clear instruction
- Safe · inclusive · no competitive shaming

## Example (theme: feeling overwhelmed)

**Notice 1:** Draw three things on your plate today. Circle one you can ask help with.

**Notice 2:** Notice where your shoulders are. Take one slow breath.

**Try:** Pick one small task. Finish it before starting the next.

## Anti-patterns

- Homework-heavy · test-like
- Requires parent to explain philosophy
- Activity unrelated to story events
